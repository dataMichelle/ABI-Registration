The tiny-print stuff

Quick answers.

Tap a question. Skip the wall of text.

Who needs to register?+

Every attending Brat and Annuitant registers individually with their own MemberLeap account.

Can I register another Brat?+

No. Each member must use their own account and complete a separate registration.

What if my membership lapsed?+

MemberLeap will prompt you to pay applicable membership fees before reunion registration.

When do I get the hotel link?+

Right after your registration payment is successfully completed.

Are add-ons included?+

No. Tournaments, brunch, swag, and suite raffle tickets are separate.
